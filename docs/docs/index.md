# Welcome to HomeGallery's documentation!

## Discover your memories

Explore your photos and videos safe from home with an open mind and free
from cloud and vendors. HomeGallery is a mobile friendly web gallery to
serve all your private media.

Please visit the [demo HomeGallery](https://demo.home-gallery.org/) and
start exploring my public photos.

:::info
HomeGallery is a pet project and was started 2019 to solve my needs and requires CLI/terminal interactions. The most tested platform is Linux/Debian. It runs also on Mac or Windows but are barley tested.
:::
